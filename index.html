We'll walk through the real workflow in about a minute.

How FollowUp works

Use FollowUp as your daily follow-up command center.

Start on Dashboard, work today's follow-ups, then use Pipeline when you want to manage deals by stage.

1

Start on Dashboard

Today shows the clients you need to contact now. Open a client or complete the follow-up from this area.

2

Complete a follow-up

When you finish a scheduled follow-up, choose Done, tell FollowUp what happened, then choose the next move.

3

Use Pipeline for deals

Click Pipeline at the top to see New, Contacted, Proposal, Won and Lost. Click a deal card to open its actions.

4

Understand Pipeline momentum

On Dashboard, click Moving, Waiting, Stalled, or Forgotten. The matching clients will appear so you can see exactly who makes up that number.

5

Reschedule or log activity

Use the deal drawer or client detail for Call, Email, Text, Meeting, or Reschedule follow-up.

6

Closed deals stay protected

Won and Lost are locked to prevent accidental changes. If you made a mistake, use Undo status change.

Quick rule: Dashboard = what should I do today?  ·  Pipeline = where are my deals?  ·  Clients = find and manage a specific client.
